Membership

Come share your knowledge and experiences with other government IT professionals, and get access to the tools members use every day.

What members get

  • Email listservAsk questions and share answers with peers across the region.
  • ForumDiscuss Spillman Flex administration and use with other members.
  • Members-only siteAccess important information shared by our members.
  • Semi-annual SNUG forumsMeet in person twice a year. See Conferences.

Who can join

Users and Admins of the CAD, Jail, Law, and Records Modules, and IT Professionals at public safety agencies using Motorola Spillman Flex in Washington, Oregon, Idaho, Montana, and Alaska.
