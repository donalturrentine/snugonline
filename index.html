Who we are and what we do

SNUG (Spillman Flex Northwest Users Group) is an organization composed of dedicated professionals from within the states of Washington, Oregon, Idaho, Montana, and Alaska that are interested in expanding their knowledge of Motorola’s Spillman Flex software.

The group consists of Users and Admins of the CAD, Jail, Law, and Records Modules, and IT Professionals that share an interest in improving their systems, and suggesting improvements to the Motorola Corporation.

Our goals

Not for profit, for public safety

SNUG is a not for profit group organized exclusively for Public Safety Agencies in Washington, Oregon and Idaho using the Motorola Spillman/Flex suite of software.

Sharing what we know

SNUG will encourage and facilitate the sharing of information relating to the administration and use of Spillman/Flex software.

Training and a stronger voice

SNUG assists to facilitate and provide training; recommend software enhancements; explore existing and potential future software interfaces; provide mutual assistance; and assist with facilitating reduced purchase prices.

Become a member

Come share your knowledge and experiences with other government IT professionals via our email listserv and forum, and access important information from our members’ only site. Be sure to join us at our semi-annual SNUG forums!
